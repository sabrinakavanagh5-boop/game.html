GUNS N FUN

VR (Quest, etc.): pick a map, then press Enter VR. Swing your arms like a gorilla: push your hands against the ground, trees, cacti and rocks to move, and let go to launch. Trigger shoots; A/B/X/Y or thumbstick click cycles that hand’s gun (finger gun, AK-47, SMG).

Flat-screen test mode: Mouse look · LMB shoot · 1 finger guns · 2 AK-47 · 3 SMG
Q/E slap ground (alternate fast to build speed), WASD steer, hold Q/E near a tree or rock to grab and climb, release to launch, Space hop, Y rotates the gorillas if they face the wrong way.
